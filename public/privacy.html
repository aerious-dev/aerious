Privacy

Last updated 31 August 2026

This site collects nothing. There is no account to make, no form to fill in, no analytics, and no cookie set by us.

aerious.co is a single page of text, video and one email link. What follows is an honest account of the only places your visit is visible at all — none of which are us.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising scripts, and no A/B testing. We set no cookies and use no local storage. We keep no server of our own, so there is no log for us to read even if we wanted one.

What our hosting sees

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser user-agent in order to deliver the page, and may retain that briefly for security and abuse prevention. This is GitHub's processing, not ours; we have no access to it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What Google sees

The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore requests files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Google states it does not use these requests to build advertising profiles. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

If you would rather not contact Google at all, a content blocker will stop the request. The page falls back to a system serif and remains fully readable.

If you email us

Writing to support@aerious.co sends your message, address and whatever you choose to include to our mailbox, which runs on Google Workspace. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent or share it. Ask us to delete the correspondence and we will.

Your rights

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA or similar law applies, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port the personal data an organisation holds about you, and to object to its processing. In our case the only data we hold is email you have sent us. Write to support@aerious.co and we will act on it.

Children

This site is not directed at children and asks nobody for personal information.

Changes

If this site ever starts collecting something — analytics, a form, an account — this page will say so before it does, and the date at the top will change.
